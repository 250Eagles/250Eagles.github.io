about

Engineering, on a clock

Every FIRST Tech Challenge season hands teams a brand-new game and a matter of weeks to design, build, wire, and program a robot capable of playing it, then compete head-to-head against other teams at live events. Team 36855 handles every part of that process ourselves: mechanical design, CAD, fabrication, electrical, and software.

Beyond the robot, we work on outreach, strategy, and the business side of running a competitive team — which is where our sponsors come in. See how a partnership works.
